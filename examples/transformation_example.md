## Step 4: Approved Reframed Output (Anti-AI-Slop Verified)

```text
I've watched good salespeople lose enterprise deals while doing exactly what their playbook taught them.

The prospect mentions a broken workflow. The seller immediately switches to screen share and shows the feature that fixes it.

They think they're demonstrating competence. What the buyer actually experiences is impatience.

When you offer a solution five seconds after hearing a problem, you signal that the problem is trivial.

In complex B2B deals, authority comes from diagnosis, not demo speed.

Before showing the solution, three questions worth asking:
1. How long has this problem existed?
2. What internal workarounds have already failed?
3. Who else in the company feels the impact when this stalls?

Have you ever seen a deal slow down because the seller moved too fast?
```
